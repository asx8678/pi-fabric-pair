## Replanning policy chosen for the next exact contract

Main selects the conservative report-boundary policy from the stopped proposal:

1. Replace current step only after actual report, closed grant admission, real native completion or sufficiently complete containment, settled admitted effects, finalized checkpoint and qualified supervisor review. Old step becomes superseded, not approved. Transfer outstanding review debt to an explicitly named successor; do not advance the approved workspace base on revise.
2. Preserve original ledgers. Replacements/splits inherit cumulative per-step consumption and unknowns; no reset by new IDs. Preserve workflow caps/deadlines/participants/workspace/config.
3. At most 32 lifetime distinct step IDs, including retired steps, not a replenished active-list allowance.
4. Permit only named atomic supersession of an uncommitted candidate for the same report. Preserve candidate identity/history; a later old commitment stays held and cannot apply.
5. No-report emergency handling is terminal cancellation/containment only in this scope. Do not fabricate a report or use terminal logical disposal to authorize nonterminal replanning.

These are policy choices, not permission to guess a kernel schema. E2 must define exact expected/next revisions, full remaining order, complete introduced step content and replacement lineage, removed IDs, current disposition, earlier producer/control/native-settlement link and commit/supersession order before editing coordination.js.
